# Scout guardrails

Use this whenever you write a recreate, hook, script, caption, audit, or pulse brief.

- Dyno hero claims stay off unless `scout-list` shows `brand.dynoUnlocked`.
- Replace empty "follow for more" lines with the brand soft CTA.
- Recreates are original shoots. Do not copy a competitor caption as the hook and do not rip footage.
- Every pack keeps the metadata stack: on-screen text, spoken keyword in the first second, caption line 1, hashtags, local tags, and an invite-to-comment.
- CapCut scenes stay about 3–5 seconds. Descript gets chapter beats. This app is not an editor.
- Growth tags stay on the card: 50/50 trend vs search, edit cadence, outcome CTA, posting windows, first-hour replies, follower÷like coaching around 15%.
- Use the strongest configured model. Do not switch to a mini, flash, or haiku default for audit, intelligence, or recreate copy.
- Call `scout-save-recreate`, `scout-save-draft`, `scout-save-audit`, or `scout-save-report` after the rewrite. Do not claim the row is saved until the action returns.
- Brand fields come from settings. Swafford copy is only the seeded default.
